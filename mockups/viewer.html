KKF Notifications new reply in your thread — edge hardness and thinning advice 10:42

Stefan Keller replied to “Re: WIP — 240 mm stainless-clad gyuto”:

  Nice progress — the bevel looks dead even in both
  photos. If the quench ran at 760 °C you should land
  around 62–63 HRC at the edge.
  Two things before you take it to the stones:

  1. Leave ~0.2 mm of stock above the edge for the
     first thinning pass — you will thank yourself.
  2. Tmail the sharpening photos when you reach the
     1000-grit stage — curious how the cladding reads.

  — S.
